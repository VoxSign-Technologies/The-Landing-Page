Questions we get asked.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. PearlEdu stores attendance and marks on the device and uploads them when the network returns, so a dropped connection never costs a teacher their work.

Which sign language do you support?

Ugandan Sign Language. It is a distinct language, not a dialect of American Sign Language, and we build it with Deaf signers here rather than importing a model trained elsewhere.

Are Accessibility and PearlEdu the same product?

No. They share a company and a mission, nothing else. Accessibility is for individual Deaf and speech-impaired users. PearlEdu is bought by a school. You can use either one on its own.

What does PearlEdu cost?

Pricing is per school and depends on enrolment. Tell us your student numbers and we will send a figure the same week.

What phones does it need?

Any Android phone from the last few years with a working camera. There is no hardware to buy and no server to run in the school.

Tell us what you need.

Schools looking for a demo, organisations working with Deaf communities, and anyone who wants to test the translation tool. We answer within two working days.
