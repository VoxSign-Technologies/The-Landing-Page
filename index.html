VoxSign Accessibility

Two tools, one job: make sure everyone is understood.

Communication gaps shut people out — at school, at work, in everyday conversation. These two accessibility tools close them from opposite directions. They are not the school management platform.

Ugandan Sign Language app

Converts spoken words into Ugandan Sign Language through a signing avatar, in real time — for classrooms, calls, and videos.

Assistive Speech Recognition

VoxSign's speech recognition tool helps people with speech impairments be understood. It is built by training OpenAI's Whisper speech-recognition model on non-standard speech patterns, so it can recognize what someone is saying even when their speech does not follow typical patterns.

For institutions

VoxSign Institutions — school management, without the spreadsheets.

Built for schools that need one system, not five disconnected ones. This is the school platform — not the sign-language or speech tools above.

Attendance

Track student attendance across classes without paper registers.

Grading

Record and report assessment results in one consistent system.

Fees

Manage school fee billing and payments, including mobile money.

Communication

Keep staff, parents, and administrators on the same page.
