VoxSign Institutions · PearlEdu

School management, built around how schools actually run.

PearlEdu is a multi-tenant school management system for Ugandan schools. Each school's data is scoped and isolated from every other school — in the application and again at the database layer.

Go to PearlEdu Request a walkthrough

What's inside

Admissions & enrollment

Applications, acceptance, guardian attachment, class assignment, and enrollment in one flow.

Attendance & assessment

Daily attendance, marks entry scoped to each teacher's assigned classes and subjects, and marksheet publication.

Fees & payments

Fee structures, invoicing, and payment tracking, with financial roles kept separate from academic ones.

Role-based workspaces

Admins, directors, head teachers, directors of studies, bursars, secretaries, teachers, parents, and students each see work scoped to their role.

Timetabling

Class and subject timetables generated and maintained per school.

Parent & student portal

Guardians and learners can view attendance, results, timetable, and fee status for their own family — and nothing beyond it.

How schools stay isolated

Many schools run on the same platform, but no school can see or modify another school's records. Access is scoped by role and school membership in the application, and enforced again by the database, so a mistake in application logic cannot expose another school's data.
